## Coverage notes & gaps

- **15+ competitors target met.** Direct e-commerce: ASOS, H&M, Mango, Net-a-Porter (+ PORTER), Farfetch, ABOUT YOU, SSENSE, Uniqlo StyleHint. Marketplace / aggregator: Lyst. Discovery-first / social: Pinterest (Shop the Look + Lens), Lemon8/TikTok Shop (treated together — see matrix row). AI styling: Stitch Fix. Wardrobe apps: Whering. Plus the Zalando baseline. That's 14 distinct competitor entities + Zalando = 15.
- **ABOUT YOU and H&M Complete the Look:** marked 🔍 in the matrix where direct PDP behavior couldn't be verified from public third-party critiques without logged-in browsing. Both are listed honestly as gaps; production research would resolve them by direct app inspection.
- **Mr Porter / Stylebook / Save Your Wardrobe / ShopStyle / Instagram Shop:** mentioned in the brief but not covered in this iteration — Mr Porter overlaps tightly with Net-a-Porter's pattern; Save Your Wardrobe and Stylebook overlap tightly with Whering's pattern; ShopStyle and Instagram Shop overlap tightly with Lyst / TikTok Shop patterns respectively. Treated as "covered by analogue" rather than duplicated.
- **TikTok Shop and Lemon8 collapsed into one matrix row** — both are creator-led UGC with product tags and similar provenance issues. Distinct platforms but the design pattern is the same; analysed jointly to keep the matrix scannable.
